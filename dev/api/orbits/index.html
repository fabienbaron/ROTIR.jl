Orbits

Kepler orbits for binary stars: positions, separations and radial velocities. Times are JD. bparams is a NamedTuple with the fields of binaryparameters below; a Roche star_params carries the same orbital fields and works too.

Kepler's equation

FunctionDescription
compute_E_NR(M, e)Solves E − e·sin(E) = M to machine precision, scalar or array M (T keyword accepted and ignored)
compute_eccentric_anomaly(bparams, tepoch)Eccentric anomaly, with the period derivative dP
compute_true_anomaly(bparams, tepoch)True anomaly, by the Broucke–Cefola formula

Orbital positions

FunctionDescription
compute_coeff(Ω, i, ω)The six direction cosines (L1, M1, N1, L2, M2, N2); angles in radians
compute_xyz_rel(a, β, e, L1, M1, N1, L2, M2, N2, cosE, sinE)Secondary relative to primary, β = √(1−e²)
binary_orbit_rel(bparams, tepoch)Relative orbit: (0, 0, 0, x, y, z)
binary_orbit_abs(bparams, tepoch)Both components about the centre of mass: (x1, y1, z1, x2, y2, z2)
binary_proj_plane(bparams, tepochs)Relative orbit on the sky: (x, y, ρ, θ), θ in radians
omega_at(bparams, tepoch)Argument of periapsis at tepoch with apsidal motion dω (radians)

Positions are in mas, with x = North, y = East, z = receding. ω is the argument of periapsis of the relative orbit; the primary's is ω + π.

Coordinate conversion

FunctionDescription
orbit_to_rotir_offset(bparams, tepoch)Secondary's offset from the primary in ROTIR's (West, North) frame, mas; scalar or vector of epochs

Separation

FunctionDescription
compute_separation(bparams, tepoch)Separation in units of a, D = 1 − e·cos(E)

Radial velocities

FunctionDescription
binary_RV(bparams, tepoch; K1, K2, γ)(Vrad1, Vrad2); positive is receding; units of K1, K2, γ

Stellar parameters

starparameters(rpole, tpole, frac_escapevel, ldtype, ld1, ld2, beta, B_rot, inclination, position_angle, rotation_offset, rotation_period) returns a NamedTuple with these fields:

FieldDescription
rpolePolar radius (mas)
tpolePolar temperature (K)
frac_escapevelΩ/Ω_crit
ldtypeLimb-darkening law (0 = none, 1 = linear, 2 = quadratic, 3 = power law, 4 = Claret)
ld1, ld2Limb-darkening coefficients
betaGravity-darkening exponent
B_rotDifferential rotation coefficient
inclinationSpin-axis inclination (degrees)
position_angleSpin-axis position angle (degrees)
rotation_offsetFixed rotation offset (degrees)
rotation_periodRotation period (days)

Binary parameters

binaryparameters(star1, star2, d, i, Ω, ω, P, a, e, T0, q, fillout_factor, dP, dω) returns a NamedTuple with these fields:

FieldDescription
star1, star2NamedTuple from starparameters() for each component
dDistance (pc)
iOrbital inclination (degrees)
ΩLongitude of the ascending node (degrees)
ωArgument of periapsis of the relative orbit (degrees)
POrbital period (days)
aSemi-major axis of the relative orbit (mas)
eEccentricity
T0Time of periastron (JD)
qMass ratio M₂/M₁
fillout_factorRoche-lobe fill-out factors [primary, secondary]
dPPeriod derivative (days/day)
dωApsidal motion (degrees/day)

Ω, ω and dω are Unicode field names; ASCII Omega, omega or domega are not read.
