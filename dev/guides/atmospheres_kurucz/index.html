Kurucz ATLAS9

Kurucz's ATLAS9 intensity packs reach 50000 K at 17 emergent angles, which covers the hot rotators (Spica, Vega, Regulus, β Lyr); Korg/MARCS stops at 8000 K. See Model atmospheres and limb darkening for the provider interface.

Getting a pack

kurucz_pack_path with download = true downloads ip00k2.pck19 (67.5 MB) once from http://kurucz.harvard.edu/grids/gridp00/ into intensity_cache_dir() (under the first Julia depot) and returns the cached path afterwards. Choosing "Kurucz pack" in the GUI does the same. Set ROTIR_KURUCZ_PACK to use an existing copy.

grid = read_kurucz_intensity(kurucz_pack_path(; download = true);
                             λrange = (1.5e-6, 1.8e-6),          # metres
                             Teff = (18000.0, 28000.0), logg = (3.0, 4.5))
prov = TabulatedProvider(grid; name = "Kurucz ATLAS9 H band")

λrange is required. A full pack is 1221 wavelengths × 17 angles × ~500 models; an H-band grid needs about 30 wavelengths. Teff and logg restrict the axes.

The pack is not a complete (Teff, logg) rectangle: missing nodes are filled from the nearest logg at the same Teff, with a warning giving the count. Restricting logg to what the surface needs avoids the filled nodes.

ip00k2new.pck (31.5 MB) has the newer ODFNEW opacities but stops at 8750 K. The reader reads both formats (they differ by one column) and converts the stored values, which are I(μ)/I(1) × 10⁵, to intensities.

Limb darkening of hot stars

A hot star is less limb-darkened in H than a warm one: $I(1)/I(0.01)$ is 1.44 at 25000 K and 1.95 at 7000 K. At 1.65 μm a 25000 K photosphere is far down the Planck tail, where the source function varies slowly with depth. Fitted limb darkening is therefore even less constrained on a hot star than on β Cas.
